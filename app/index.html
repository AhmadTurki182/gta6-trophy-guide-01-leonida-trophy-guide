Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Stage 1 — Story playthrough. Play the main story from Gone Fishing to the final mission. Grab the missable trophies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Gunslinger, Catch Me If You Can, Sneakthief, Ten Pin) as their moments come up, and pick up stranger strands whenever you pass a marker.
  2. Stage 2 — Side content. Finish every stranger and contact strand (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), try all 9 activity types, and win your first races and drone races.
  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles & grinds. Shooting Range golds, 250 stunts, 100 vehicles, card sets, WAiNK It spots, meditation spots, all 52 areas, bank vaults, and the money trophies.
  4. Stage 4 — Cleanup & platinum. Chase 100% completion for A Long Road, mop up anything left, and the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — unlocks itself.
