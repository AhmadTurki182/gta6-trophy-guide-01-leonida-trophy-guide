Roadmap to the Platinum

Order built from the official descriptions: story chapters first, then side stories, then open-world cleanup, then 100% completion. About 200 hours in total — long, not hard. No difficulty trophies, no mission medals.

THE missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots). The Chapter 1 balcony spot — Lucia, the morning after “Down to Earth”, on the balcony of Jason’s house — disappears once Chapter 1 ends. Do it before the chapter closes and keep a Chapter 1 save until it pops.
Save discipline. No mission replay, no New Game Plus. You get 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave: keep a save at the start of every chapter and one just before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats switch trophies and saving off. Yago’s street-racing strand (part of Strangers & Freaks) is time-sensitive — save first, race at once.
