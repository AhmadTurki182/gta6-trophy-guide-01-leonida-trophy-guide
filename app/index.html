About this list

Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft have not published the trophy list themselves. The names, descriptions and grades here come from the trackers Exophase and PSNProfiles, as reported on 18 November 2026, which the competition kit treats as the official list. Every guide below is written in our own words and names its source beside it; no guide text is copied from another site.

All 55 trophies are single-player. There are no online trophies. Xbox has the same 55 achievements for 1,000 Gamerscore. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so the roadmap below matters.

Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

  1. Stage 1 — Save discipline first. There are 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a save at the start of every chapter and one just before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save: cheats switch off trophies and saving there.
  2. Stage 2 — The missable one. Meditate at the balcony of Jason’s house on the morning after “Down to Earth” in Chapter 1. That spot vanishes when Chapter 1 ends, and with it the Positive Mental Attitude trophy. Do it early and keep your Chapter 1 save until it pops.
  3. Stage 3 — Story, Chapters 1–4 and the finale. Twelve story trophies: the opening mission, the four chapter trophies, the final mission, and six mission trophies. All unmissable — just play. Race Yago’s strand in Chapter 3 at once when it appears; it runs on a timer.
  4. Stage 4 — Strangers and side strands. Eight stranger strands for Strangers & Freaks (Andy, Billy and Tyrone do not count — they have their own trophies), plus the six side-character finales. Everything here stays open after the credits.
  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup to 100% and platinum. Stunts (250 for the trophy, 300 for 100%), 42 Shooting Range golds, 14 drone races, 8 bank vaults, 39 illegal operations, 200 of 277 vehicles, 70 of 80 challenges, and the money trophies. The 100% checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story.

Export & import

Export writes a small JSON file with the names of the trophies you have ticked. Import reads such a file back and restores your ticks. The file never leaves your computer unless you share it yourself.
