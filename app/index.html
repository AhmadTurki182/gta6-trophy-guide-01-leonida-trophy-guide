Trophy checklist

    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough: there are no difficulty trophies, no online trophies and no New Game Plus. There is also no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission. Cheats switch trophies off, so leave them alone on a trophy run. About 200 hours in all, most of it after the credits.

    About this guide

    Names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them; the trophy list was taken from Exophase and cross-checked against PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements. Each guide is written in our own words and names its sources beside it, so you can see where every fact comes from — a guide never copies another guide's text. Where the exact steps of a trophy are not confirmed yet, the guide says so instead of guessing.

    This guide is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games and uses no Rockstar logo, artwork, box art or game screenshots. Progress is stored only in this browser (localStorage); export and import move it as a file you own.
